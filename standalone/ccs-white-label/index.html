It takes less than 30 seconds to calculate your Child Care Subsidy. Find out how much your family could claim.

The Child Care Subsidy (CCS) is a government payment available to families to help cover the costs of childcare. The subsidy is paid directly to your child’s early learning provider to reduce the amount of fees that you pay out-of-pocket.

Your out-of-pocket costs depend on how much CCS you are entitled to, depending on your family’s individual situation. If your circumstances change, it’s important to update your details to ensure you continue to receive the best support, entitlements, and care possible.

Frequently Asked Questions from Parents

Am I eligible for the Child Care Subsidy (CCS)?

To qualify for CCS, families must meet the following criteria:

1. Residency and Immunisation

  • You meet the Australian residency rules.
  • Your child must meet immunisation requirements and not be attending secondary school unless an exemption applies.
  • You pay fees at an approved childcare centre.

For further information, please refer to this CCS information.

2. Additional Support

Some families may be eligible for additional subsidy benefits. Eligibility criteria include:

  • Being a grandparent
  • Experiencing temporary financial hardship
  • Transitioning to work
  • Caring for a vulnerable or at-risk child

For further information, please refer to ACCS information.

3. Types of Approved Childcare Services Covered

The subsidy applies only to approved childcare services such as those offered at all Busy Bees centres.

How much Child Care Subsidy will I receive?

The amount of CCS you can receive depends on four factors:

  • Your family’s income
  • The hourly rate cap based on the type of approved childcare you use and your child’s age
  • The hours of recognised activity you and your partner do
  • The number of children in your care

The amount of subsidised childcare you can access per fortnight applies to each child. If your income or activity changes, update your details with Centrelink to ensure you receive the correct CCS amount.

How do I claim the Child Care Subsidy?

Applying for CCS is simple. You can apply online through your myGov account linked to Centrelink. Here’s how:

  1. Apply for your Customer Reference Number (CRN) from Centrelink.
  2. Create a myGov account if you don’t already have one.
  3. Once signed in, go to Centrelink > Payments and claims > Make a claim > Families > Child Care Subsidy.
  4. Complete your Child Care Subsidy assessment, then submit the claim.
  5. Once approved, the subsidy will be paid directly to your childcare provider.
How does the CCS calculator work?

It takes under a minute to find out what portion of subsidised childcare you may be eligible for. The calculator provides an indicative guide to your out-of-pocket costs.

Step 1: Service Information – Enter details about the Busy Bees centre you wish to attend. The calculator estimates your weekly and fortnightly out-of-pocket costs based on fees and hours in care.

Step 2: Activity Test – Determine if you meet the recognised activity test requirements. The calculator uses your answers to estimate the portion of CCS you can receive.

Step 3: Family Information – Enter how many children will be in care. If you don’t know your CCS percentage, the calculator can estimate it using your activity test answers and taxable income.

What’s the hourly rate cap, and how does it affect me?

The hourly rate cap is the maximum amount the government will subsidise per hour, per child, based on the type of care you use. If your provider charges above the cap, you pay the difference. As of 2025, the caps are:

  • Centre-based day care: $13.17 per hour
  • Family day care: $11.73 per hour
  • Outside school hours care: $13.17 per hour

These caps are indexed annually, so they may increase slightly each year.

Can I get CCS for multiple children?

Yes. Families with more than one child under five can receive a higher CCS rate for younger children enrolled in care.

What if my income changes?

Keep your details up to date. Changes to income, activity level or family situation can affect how much CCS you receive. Update your information through your myGov account to avoid being overpaid or underpaid.

Can I switch childcare providers and still receive CCS?

Yes. If you switch to a Busy Bees centre, your CCS automatically transfers once your new enrolment is confirmed.
